# Arena of Valor historical roster research

Scope: male Arena of Valor (AoV) international 5v5 events only. Honor of Kings (HOK), women-only tournaments, 1v1 showmatches, and qualifier-only events not in the named edition are excluded. This document distinguishes event participants from registered/played players; a player must not be added to the canonical rosters without event-specific evidence.

## Verification policy

- `verified`: the exact fact is explicitly supported by an event-specific page or official event record and, where possible, independently corroborated.
- `partial`: only some fields are supported; unverified fields remain blank.
- `unverified`: a search lead only; do not treat as canonical data or as confirmed registration/participation.
- Event participation does not prove a player was registered; a registered roster does not prove match appearance. Use separate registration and played evidence in `rosters.csv`.
- Do not infer roles, legal names, nationalities, or Head Coach status from team membership or historic rosters.
- No images were downloaded or reused; image rights have not been researched.

## Research integrity note

Search-result summaries collected during this pass contain material conflicts in team names, player-team assignments, role assignments, roster size, and even tournament-stage participation. They are leads, not sufficient evidence for bulk import into the canonical CSVs. The requested 2018–2025 player additions have therefore not been promoted to `players.csv` or `rosters.csv` until each row can be checked against the event-specific Liquipedia page (and a reliable cross-check when available). No names from a previous season should be carried forward to fill gaps.
